## 📱 DEMO 1: WhatsApp Experience (3 minutes)

### Setup
Show the WhatsApp interface on screen (can use a mock or real WhatsApp)

### Script

**Presenter:**
> "Let me show you how the AI Tutor helps a learner - not by giving answers, but by understanding their thinking."

---

#### Step 1: First Contact

**[Send message: "Hi"]**

**AI Tutor responds:**
> 👋 Hello! Welcome to AI Tutor! 🎓
> 
> I'm your personal Mathematics tutor. I'll help you learn to solve problems step by step.
> 
> First, tell me your grade. Reply with:
> • Grade 1 (or just type: grade 1)
> • Grade 2
> • ...
> • Grade 12

**Presenter:**
> "The tutor personalizes the experience based on grade level."

---

#### Step 2: Set Grade

**[Send message: "Grade 9"]**

**AI Tutor responds:**
> ✅ Grade 9 set!
> 
> Now you'll get questions and hints suited for your level.
> 
> What would you like to learn today?

**Presenter:**
> "Now let's see the reasoning analysis in action."

---

#### Step 3: The Problem

**[Send message: "Help me solve: 2x + 5 = 13"]**

**AI Tutor responds:**
> Great question! Let's solve this together. 🎯
> 
> I won't give you the answer - instead, I'll help YOU find it.
> 
> First, tell me: What do you think is the goal when solving this equation?

**Presenter:**
> "Notice - it asks the learner to think, not just provide steps."

---

#### Step 4: The Misconception (KEY MOMENT!)

**[Send message: "I think I should add 13 and 5 together"]**

**AI Tutor responds:**
> Interesting thinking! Let me help you see this differently.
> 
> An equation is like a balance scale ⚖️. You have 2x + 5 on the left, and 13 on the right.
> 
> If you add 13 and 5, which side would change?
> 
> 🤔 Think about it: We want to get x by itself. What's 'in the way' of x?

**Presenter:**
> "This is the key innovation. The AI detected a misconception - the learner wants to combine numbers across the equals sign. Instead of just saying 'wrong', it guides them to understand equation balance."

---

#### Step 5: The Correction

**[Send message: "Oh, I should subtract 5 from both sides"]**

**AI Tutor responds:**
> Exactly right! 🌟
> 
> When you subtract 5 from both sides:
> 2x + 5 - 5 = 13 - 5
> 2x = 8
> 
> Great job! Now, what's the next step to find x?

**Presenter:**
> "The learner now understands WHY - not just WHAT to do."

---

#### Step 6: Success

**[Send message: "Divide by 2, so x = 4"]**

**AI Tutor responds:**
> Perfect! You've got it! 🎉
> 
> 2x ÷ 2 = 8 ÷ 2
> x = 4
> 
> Let's check: 2(4) + 5 = 8 + 5 = 13 ✓
> 
> You solved it! The key skills you used:
> 1. Maintaining balance (same operation on both sides)
> 2. Using inverse operations (subtract to undo addition)
> 
> Would you like to try another problem?

**Presenter:**
> "The learner solved it themselves. That's real learning."
